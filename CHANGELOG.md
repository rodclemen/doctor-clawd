# Changelog

Clawd Clash brings three apps together as its three universes: Clawd Wars became Star Clawds, Avengers: Clawd Wars became Marvelous Clawds and Justice League: Clawd Wars became Detective Clawds. Their history before that is in [history/star-clawds.md](history/star-clawds.md) (up to 2.28.3), [history/marvelous-clawds.md](history/marvelous-clawds.md) (0.1.0) and [history/detective-clawds.md](history/detective-clawds.md) (0.1.0).

## Unreleased

### Added
- **A fourth universe, Doctor Clawd.** Settings → Universe → Doctor Clawd, and in the screen saver's Options. 98 characters: all fifteen numbered Doctors plus the War and Fugitive Doctors, each with their own sonic screwdriver colour (or cricket ball, umbrella or Venusian aikido), 38 companions and allies from Susan Foreclaw to Belinda Chandler, K-Canine gliding on his base, and HEM in a fez; and 42 villains, from the Master in five faces, Missy, Davross and the Dalick Emperor to the Cyberpersons, Sontarons, Silents, Zygones, Slitheem and the Candyman, with Adipoze, Cybermites and Toclafayne turning up as agents. 20 places, from the TARDIS console room, Gallifray and Skarro to Totter's Yard, the Library, Starship UK, Pompeii, the café in Arles under its swirling sky and Sanctuary Base 6 under its black hole, each with what moves there. Arch-enemies face off first (Ten and Mister Sexton, Twelve and Missy, Amy and the Weeping Angle…). Signature moves where the show has one: Four's jelly babies, Eleven's fez, Jack dropping dead and gasping back, Rose's Bad Wolf glow, Wilf's salute, Osgoode's inhaler.
- **Doctor Clawd's weapons follow the show.** The Snakeskin Master spits venom, the Spy Master carries the tissue compression eliminator, Rascilon's gauntlet fires a beam, the Valeyarn (no powers of his own) has a pistol, the Toclafayne fire lasers, the Odd's lightning comes from its translation sphere, Captain Jack fires bullets from his revolver, and the Dalick Emperor never fires at all. The lightning left is for those who throw it on screen: Davross's fingers, the Silents, the Zygones' stingers, the Sycorex whip.
- **The TARDIS.** Doctors and companions leave in a police box that wheezes in and out of view where they stand, lamp flashing, door swinging open on the light inside, instead of a ship flying in.
- **Doctor Clawd's own transitions**, built on how the show itself gets about: the TARDIS dematerialising out of the old place and into the new, and each era's title sequence: the 1963 howlaround (black-and-white TV feedback), the 1970s slit-scan tunnel, the 1980s starfield, the 2005 time vortex with the TARDIS tumbling through it, the 2010s explosive vortex and today's neon one. Each era's comes up more often with its Doctors and companions on screen (the howlaround with One and Two or at Totter's Yard, the neon vortex with Thirteen to Fifteen…). A regeneration's golden flare can come up any time, more often with a Doctor about. Blink (an Angle closer every time the screen blinks) and the view through a Dalick's eyestalk aren't how the show travels, so they only come up with an Angle or a Dalick on screen (or on Skarro and at the Asylum). `Tools/Transitions` renders all ten between two Doctor Clawd places.
- **Regeneration.** When a Claude Code session in Doctor Clawd compacts its context, its Doctor regenerates into the next one in a golden flare: same session, new face. It needs Claude Code's `PreCompact` hook, which Clawd Clash now connects too (reconnect from Settings, or rebuild, to add it).
- **The Weeping Angle only moves while nobody is looking at it**: frozen whenever anyone on stage faces it, then quick when everyone has turned away.
- **Practice of their own**: Dalicks "Elevate!" a step off the ground, Cyberpersons march in step, the Sontaron drills, Four tosses jelly babies, Twelve plays the guitar with notes flying off it, and K-Canine plays fetch. Nobody in Doctor Clawd floats cross-legged in meditation: a sonic screwdriver isn't the Force.
- **The Doctor Clawd page**: the README now shows off Doctor Clawd the way the Star Clawds page does, with its own icon, the desktop, the TARDIS, Weeping Angle and regeneration strips, the cast, window mode in the console room and on Skarro, the twenty places, the ten transitions, the screen saver and a hover card. `Tools/RenderShowcase` draws the icon and the strips from the app's own sprites; the window, screen saver and hover pictures are `CLAWD_SHOT` captures of a Playground run.
- **A Doctor Clawd trailer**: `REEL=doctor build/renderpromo video promo` renders `promo/doctor-clawd-promo.mp4`. It opens on a title sequence of its own (lightning, then down a pixel-art time vortex with the police box tumbling past and DOCTOR CLAWD swelling out of its eye), then a squadron of Dalicks drops onto a winter street in London shrieking EXTERMINATE! until the TARDIS wheezes in under their fire and Doctor Ten's sonic sends them spinning off. The tune is written for it: a galloping plucked bass, swells of filtered wind and a swooping sine lead, the old electronic theme's sounds but not its melody. The invasion scene's score hits harder: a kick under the bass, a pad of minor chords underneath, and a louder EXTERMINATE!, Dalick fire, TARDIS wheeze and sonic. The trailer's window and fight shots bring in Rose, Donna, River, a Cyberperson and a Sontaron instead of repeating Ten and a Dalick. The blooper reel's `groundRow` is renamed `stageGroundRow` so it doesn't clash with the app's own.
- **The trailer is on the README**, at the top of the Doctor Clawd page, with a note that GitHub starts videos muted.
- **Doctor Clawd's sheets**: `Tools/RenderDocs` draws `roster-doctor-clawd.png`, `locations-doctor-clawd.png`, a desktop line-up and a social preview.

### Changed
- **Test runs leave the hook's universe alone.** A Playground, demo, chaos or `CLAWD_SHOT` run no longer writes its universe to the status file at launch, so taking screenshots in another universe doesn't change who the installed app casts new sessions as. The Playground's sample prompts are Doctor Clawd's own in Doctor Clawd.
- **The TARDIS is 25% bigger** (22×35 pixels instead of 18×28, on a canvas of its own), so it stands a head taller than whoever boards it, and they walk up to its open door instead of the spot where a ship's ramp would be.
- **Blink's last Angle is smaller**, so it no longer runs off the bottom of the screen.
- **The Doctor Clawd README points at this repo.** It said the code was being built on Clawd Clash's `doctor_who` branch; the work now happens here, on a copy of Clawd Clash with Doctor Clawd as its fourth universe.
- **The README goes by the universes' names throughout**, its pictures included (`docs/roster-star-clawds.png` and so on, as `Tools/RenderDocs` now names them).
- **The source folders go by the universes' names too**: `Sources/App/Universes/StarClawds`, `MarvelousClawds` and `DetectiveClawds`, with file names to match. `Tools/Preview` takes characters and places by the names shown on screen (`build/preview cast out.png "Darth Vapor"`) and universes by theirs.

## 3.0.0 (2026-10-14)

### Added
- **The universes' own pages are named after them.** The three old repos are now [star-clawds](https://github.com/rodclemen/star-clawds), [marvelous-clawds](https://github.com/rodclemen/marvelous-clawds) and [detective-clawds](https://github.com/rodclemen/detective-clawds) (the old links still lead there), and so are the Homebrew casks that start Clawd Clash in each: `brew upgrade` moves `clawd-wars`, `clawdvengers-clawd-wars` and `justice-clawds-clawd-wars` over by itself.
- **Solo practice.** A Claude working with nobody to fight works through practice sets instead of firing straight ahead at whoever stands next to it (often someone asleep): kata, shots and spells into the sky, a portal that sometimes fizzles, meditating in the air, squats, stretches, its signature move, and for twelve characters a move of their own. Star-Laird, Deadpond and Harold the Duck moonwalk when idle.
- **Closing the window can hide instead of quit** (Settings → Window).
- **More characters and animations**, here and there across the three universes.
- **The Detective Clawds trailer, remade.** `REEL=detective` renders the old Justice League trailer again on Clawd Clash with today's names, art and logos: "MEANWHILE, AT THE HALL OF CLAWDS...", the Night Beacon's cowl mask switching to DETECTIVE CLAWDS with its D and C at full strength (and on the end card, which now points to Clawd Clash), the horned kaiju in Megalopolis, the line-up at The Lookout, and the window in The Night Grotto. The Detective Clawds column of the README's trailers, and the trailer on the justice-clawds-clawd-wars page, now show it.
- **The screen saver's clock can be turned off.** The screen saver's **Options…** sheet in System Settings gains **Show the clock**, on by default. Off, the big time and date top right are gone and the place name keeps its corner swaps as before. It's saved with the universe pick in the screen saver's own options file, so it takes effect within a second, and the app's window (which never showed a clock) is unchanged.
- **The Marvelous Clawds trailer, remade.** `REEL=marvelous` renders the old Avengers trailer again on Clawd Clash with today's names, art and logos: MARVELOUS CLAWDS on the comic-panel title and the end card (which now points to Clawd Clash), the Clawdjet flyover, and the space whale no longer cut off at the top as it dives out of the portal. The Marvelous Clawds column of the README's trailers, and the trailer on the clawdvengers-clawd-wars page, now show it.
- **The Star Clawds trailer, remade.** `REEL=starclawds` renders the old Clawd Wars trailer again on Clawd Clash, with today's names, art and logos (STAR CLAWDS on the logo and end card, the end card pointing to Clawd Clash), and a new scene aboard the Tentative IV: the blast door glows and blows in, Stormtroupers pour through the smoke trading shots with Wedge Antlers and Cassius Andoor, Princess Laya and the droids slip away, and Darth Vapor steps through after them. The Star Clawds column of the README's trailers, and the trailer on the clawd-wars page, now show it.
- **A Clawd Clash trailer and a blooper reel**, rendered by `Tools/RenderPromo` from the app's own sprites, places and transitions, each with a synthesized soundtrack. The trailer (70 s) opens on a typed comic caption and the icon's three panels with CLAWD CLASH slamming in, picks a universe on one comic page with the Settings picker underneath, gives each universe a short showcase (the giant ship over the desert planet, the space whale out of the portal, the horned kaiju in Megalopolis), then shows status, subagents, the window and Random, changing universe with the app's own transitions. The blooper reel (43.5 s) is seven takes where the universes get mixed up (the Bulk in the cantina, Darth Vapor's big reveal to Spider-Clawd, Yodle lifting Thorr's hammer…), each ending on CUT! and a slate. `REEL=promo`, `bloopers` or `full` (both back to back) picks the video; `check` renders every frame once before an export. `720p` writes the copy GitHub plays inline, sized to stay under its 10 MB limit. The README now shows the full cut where the social preview was, above the three single-universe trailers; its first frame is the CLAWD CLASH cover, so GitHub's thumbnail shows the title instead of black. The old single-universe renderer it replaces was still the Clawdvengers trailer.
- **Random universe.** **Random** (Settings → Universe) changes universe on its own every 1, 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes, to one of the other two; picking a universe stops it. In the window, a change of universe (Random's, or one you pick) now plays over the whole window, header and cards too, with the new universe's transition: one of Marvel's or DC's comic-book transitions, or Star Wars' edge or clock wipe. The crew of the new universe is already walking in underneath.
- **A third universe: DC.** Justice League: Clawd Wars joins as **DC** (Settings → Universe): 110 heroes and 97 villains, from the Justice League, the Bat-family and the Lanterns to Batman's and Superman's rogues, Apokolips and the Legion of Doom, and 43 places from the Watchtower and the Batcave to Oa and the Hall of Doom, every one drawn exactly as in Justice League: Clawd Wars. The screen saver's Options gain **DC**, and Random picks from all three. - **Two universes, one app.** Pick **Star Wars** or **Marvel** from the new **Universe** menu. A scene only ever shows one universe: switching recasts everyone (each character becomes the one in the same place on the same side of the other cast), moves the scene to one of the new universe's places, changes the menu bar icon (Vader's helmet or Captain America's shield) and the Status Symbols character, and new sessions and agents are cast from it. Star Wars brings Clawd Wars' 81 characters and 22 places; Marvel brings 193 characters and 43 places.
- **The screen saver's universe**: the screen saver's **Options…** button in System Settings picks the same universe as the app, Star Wars, Marvel, or Random (a new pick on each trip somewhere new). With several displays, each picks its own on Random.
- **Each universe keeps its own rules.** Star Wars: Han and Greedo face off first and Han shoots first, the Falcon picks Han up, whoever came first keeps their side when two AIs run, and Vader and Kylo never lead back to back. Marvel: arch-enemies face off first, the Quinjet and the Milano pick people up, flyers take to the air, and Claude Code plays the heroes when another AI runs.
- **The window steps aside when no AI is running.** In window mode, a few seconds after the last session ends and its crew has left, the window fades away instead of showing an empty scene, and it fades back (without taking focus) when an AI starts. Clicking the Dock icon, showing Clawd Clash from the menu bar icon or choosing Window Mode brings it back, and it then stays until something has run and finished again. A warning to read (an AI not connected, the status file unreadable) keeps it up.
- **Keep Characters on Top** (Settings, desktop mode): on, the crew stands above every window as before; off, it lives on the desktop itself, just above the icons and behind your windows. In window mode the same item is still Keep Window on Top.
- **A new icon**: a comic cover split into three slanted panels, one per universe (Sith red with stars, Marvel red and blue, Gotham night under the Bat-Signal), with CLAWD CLASH in big block letters across all three. Each panel colours its part of the name and the gutters cut through the letters. Spider-Man hangs upside down on his web over the name, Darth Vader raises his red saber and Batman squares up, all drawn by `Tools/MakeIcon` with the app's own sprites.
- **A screen-saver thumbnail for both universes**: the Clawd Clash title over Luke and Vader, Captain America and Thanos, in System Settings' screen saver list (it was the Avengers one).
- **Comic-book transitions in Marvel.** Moving to a new place in the Marvel universe (in the window and the screen saver) now uses a comic-book transition, a different one each time, instead of Star Wars' edge and clock wipes: halftone dots printing in the new place, a sound effect, three comic panels slamming down, a speed-line zoom through a white flash, or an iris closing on a "MEANWHILE, IN WAKANDA…" caption box. The sound effect belongs to someone on screen, each in its own look: SNIKT! (Wolverine, X-23) rips the old place open with three claw slashes, BAMF! (Nightcrawler) is a puff of purple brimstone, KRAKATOOM! or THOOM! (Thor) a storm-blue flash forking with lightning, THWIP! (any Spider-Man) a red web spinning out, BAROOM! (Hulk, the Thing) a green and orange gamma blast throwing rubble, and with none of them about, the classic yellow and red KAPOW! starburst. With Deadpool on screen he often takes over with two of his own: he climbs into the corner, tells you he's changing the set, and peels the old place off the screen like a comic page; or he surfs across on a giant chimichanga yelling CHIMICHANGAAAAA!, the new place in his wake. His captions turn up too ("MEANWHILE, ON A SMALLER BUDGET…"). Star Wars keeps its wipes. `Tools/Transitions` renders every transition as a clip and a sheet of stills (`FRAMES="key:0.3,0.6"` for single frames).

### Fixed
- **Qwen Code and Gemini CLI hook hints name the right hook again**: they said to enable `clawd-wars`, but the hook is called `clawd-clash`.
- **The screen saver's caption when no AI is running.** With no AI sessions running and Clawd Clash closed, the screen saver shows a made-up crew of locals, captioned CLAWD WARS ISN'T RUNNING · OPEN IT TO BRING YOUR CREW. That named the old app, and it was misleading: the screen saver works without the app (it reads the hooks), and opening the app brings no crew while no AI runs. It now reads NO AI SESSIONS RUNNING · THE LOCALS ARE HOLDING THE FORT.
- **A build run from source replacing your installed screen saver.** A copy of Clawd Clash run from `build/` (`./build.sh --no-install`) swapped the screen saver in `~/Library/Screen Savers` for its own build, as an app update would. It now leaves it alone, as it already left the AIs' hooks.
- **The screen saver's Options only ever keeping the first pick.** After installing, the universe chosen in Options stuck, and later picks were ignored until the screen saver was removed and installed again. macOS keeps the screen-saver host running between uses, and on recent versions a setting saved through ScreenSaverDefaults by one of its processes isn't seen by another that's already running. Options are now saved in a small file of the screen saver's own (`Clawd Clash Saver/options.json` in the host's Application Support), read afresh each time, so a new pick shows within a second.
- **Two Star Wars blasters firing the wrong shot.** Every Star Wars gun fired the same red bolt. Sebulba now fires the flame jet he used on Anakin from his pod racer, and the Jawa's ion blaster fires a blue ion bolt, like the one that drops R2 on Tatooine.
- **Marvel powers that looked wrong.** A check of all 193 Marvel characters' weapons: only Storm, Electro and Wiccan, who really throw lightning, still crackle with it. Thanos (the Power Stone), Doctor Doom, Dormammu, Mephisto, the Mandarin, Baron Mordo, Annihilus, Doctor Voodoo, Malekith and Mister Sinister now fire a steady stream of their colour instead of forked lightning. Emma Frost, a telepath with no blasts to throw, no longer fires a beam: she turns to diamond and fights hand to hand. The Leader was listed as "The MarvelVillain" (in the app and the README's roster image), left over from a rename.
- **A clean build from source**: `./build.sh` no longer warns about an unused value in the Deadpool fourth-wall transition (`ComicWipes.swift`).
- **The screen saver showing the wrong universe, or flicking between both.** The universe is now picked in one place, the screen saver's own Options: the app's Settings menu had its own pick, which always won, so choosing Marvel in Options still showed Star Wars. And with Random, the copies of the screen saver macOS runs side by side (one per display, one for System Settings' preview) each rolled their own universe and kept overwriting each other's, frame by frame; each now keeps its own.
- **The screen saver vanishing from System Settings' list.** On macOS 14 and later, the list of third-party screen savers comes from a background process (WallpaperLegacyExtension) that remembers what it found. A saver removed and put back while that process ran (a reinstall, an update, a removal) stayed missing from the list, although it was installed in the right place (`~/Library/Screen Savers`). Installing, updating and removing the screen saver now restart that process along with the screen saver host, so the list is rebuilt each time.

- **The rendered promo videos stay out of git.** The whole `promo/` folder (the trailers, the blooper reel, the full cut, their storyboards and the 720p copies) is now ignored, not only `promo/720p/`. That's about 363 MB of video that `Tools/RenderPromo` can render again at any time, so it doesn't belong in the repo's history. The 720p copies are still uploaded to GitHub by hand for the README.
- **Clawd in the character names too.** The puns on "claw" become puns on Clawd: Captain Americlawd, Luke Skyclawder, the Batclawd family (with Ace the Batclawd Hound), The Mandoclawdrian, Clawda, Mr. Fanclawdstic, Nightclawdler, Doctor Octoclawd, Apoclawdlypse, Clawdverine and Sabreclawd; the places Clawda Sandcrawler, Clawdtans Tower and Apocaclawds; and the Clawdjet, the Clawdwing and the clawdarang. The Clawdvengers emblem on Clawdvengers Tower, the Compound and the Battle of New York tower is a C in a ring with a bar shooting out through it, instead of three claw slashes. Every sheet, window shot, the hover card and the screen saver are re-rendered.
- **Clawd, not claw, in the big names.** Few of the cast have claws, but every one of them is Clawd, so the names lean on that: the universes are **Star Clawds**, **Marvelous Clawds** and **Detective Clawds** (Settings, the screen saver's Options, the menus), the teams the **Clawdvengers** and the **Justice Clawds**, with Clawdvengers Tower and Compound, the Hall of Clawds, Captain Marvelous and Ms. Marvelous to match. The showcase pages are now Clawdvengers: Clawd Wars and Justice Clawds: Clawd Wars, and their casks `clawdvengers-clawd-wars` and `justice-clawds-clawd-wars`. The place sheets, the Marvelous Clawds roster and the social previews are re-rendered with the new names.
- **Homebrew names without the originals.** The casks that start Clawd Clash in Marvelous Clawds and Detective Clawds are `clawdvengers-clawd-wars` and `justice-clawds-clawd-wars` (they were going to be `avengers-clawd-wars` and `justice-league-clawd-wars`, never published), matching the renamed repos, and every cask's description uses the universe names. `clawd-wars` and `clawd-clash` keep their names.
- **The Batclawd family's emblem is a cowl mask.** The three claw slashes that stood in for the bat are gone: Batclawd, Batclawd Girl, Batclawd Woman, Red Hood's stand-in and The Foundling wear a cowl mask (two pointed ears over a domino mask, eyes cut out) on their chests, and the same mask shines in the Night Beacon over Gothamburg and Acme Chemicals, in the Commissioner's searchlight, in the Night Beacon transition, on the Clawdwing's wing, on the app icon and on the social preview. Batclawd's batarang is a three-pointed clawdarang now too, like Batclawd Woman's.
- **Pictures re-rendered after the last design changes.** The rosters, place sheets, desktop strips, social previews, hover card, screen saver and cantina window are drawn again from the current code, so they show Batclawd Woman's new chest mark, Sinistro's lamp and the other late changes. The Justice Clawds social preview's title now shrinks to fit instead of running off both edges, and the Playground's sample prompt asks for a snowtaunt rather than naming the original creature.
- **The last bat, lantern and JL marks in Detective Clawds.** Batclawd Woman wears the family's mark in red instead of the big red bat, like the rest of the Batclawd family, and throws a three-pointed clawdarang instead of a batarang. Sinistro's chest carries the yellow lamp emblem of the other Corps, the Lantern memorial in Shore City has a green lamp on its plinth instead of the lantern circle, the Clawdwing's wing shows the family's mark in black in its yellow oval, and the Lance's tail fin reads CL instead of JL. The bat on the Batcave's computer screen and the Batmobile's fins stay as they are.
- **A Settings window, and a shorter menu.** **Settings…** (also ⌘, in the app menu) opens a window with every option in one place, with checkboxes, pop-ups and buttons: General (universe and Random, Dock icon, monochrome menu bar icon, launch at login, **Forget Idle Sessions**), Window Mode, Desktop Mode (size, characters on top, **Reset Position**), the AIs (each with Connect or Remove) and the screen saver. Window and desktop settings show side by side, whichever mode you're in. The right-click and menu bar menu keeps only Show/Hide, the mode, Location, Status Symbols, Settings… and Quit; the universe is picked in Settings.
- **The Star Wars cast is Clawd Wars' own, ported frame by frame.** All 81 characters were rebuilt on the shared pipeline and checked pixel by pixel against Clawd Wars' drawing code in hundreds of poses each. Star Wars also keeps Clawd Wars' gun handling: no gun raised while waiting or cheering, heroes check their guns without raising them, the flash stays at the resting muzzle, bolts run as they did, and each hero keeps its own practice rhythm (`Persona.soloFire`). The Emperor's crackle is pale blue and white again.
- **One engine for both**: every character in both universes is a `Persona` drawn through the same pose pipeline (`Pose.swift`), and each universe is a `Theme` in its own folder (`Sources/App/Universes/StarWars`, `.../Marvel`).
- **Upgrading from Clawd Wars or Avengers: Clawd Wars takes care of itself.** On launch, Clawd Clash takes over every AI the old app had connected: hooks that still run the old app's hook, and the AIs a Homebrew upgrade of the old cask disconnected. The old app, if it's still running, is quit. Clawd Clash starts in the universe of the app it replaces, and Star Wars keeps Clawd Wars' cast ids. (A copy run from `build/` doesn't take over; its banner says the AI still reports to the old app and to connect it from the menu.)
- **Menu**: "Show on Desktop" and "Show in Window" are now **Desktop Mode** and **Window Mode**, and **Universe** sits under Status Symbols.
- **New identity**: `Clawd Clash.app`, `com.rodclemen.clawdclash`, the `clawd-clash-hook` binary and `~/.claude/clawd-clash`.
