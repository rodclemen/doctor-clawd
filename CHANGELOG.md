## Clawd Clash 3.1.0 (2026-10-15)

- **A new universe.** Doctor Clawd joins Star Clawds, Marvelous Clawds and Detective Clawds: pick it in **Settings → Universe**, or in the screen saver's Options.
- **98 characters.** All fifteen numbered Doctors plus the War and Fugitive Doctors, each with their own sonic screwdriver (or cricket ball, umbrella or Venusian aikido); 38 companions and allies from Susan Foreclaw to Belinda Chandler; K-Canine on his base; HEM in a fez. Facing them, 42 villains: the Master in five faces, Missy, Davross, the Dalick Emperor, Cyberpersons, Sontarons, Silents, Zygones, Slitheem, the Candyman, and Adipoze, Cybermites and Toclafayne as agents.
- **20 places**, from the TARDIS console room, Gallifray and Skarro to Totter's Yard, the Library, Starship UK, Pompeii, the café in Arles and Sanctuary Base 6, each with something moving in it.
- **The TARDIS.** Doctors and companions don't fly off: a police box wheezes into view where they stand, they walk in through the door, and it fades away.
- **Ten transitions** built on how the show travels: the TARDIS dematerialising, each era's title sequence (the 1963 howlaround, the 1970s slit-scan tunnel, the 1980s starfield, the 2005 time vortex, the 2010s explosive vortex, today's neon one), a regeneration, and, with an Angle or a Dalick about, Blink or the view through a Dalick's eyestalk.
- **Regeneration.** When a Claude Code session compacts its context, its Doctor regenerates into the next one in a golden flare.
- **The Weeping Angle** only moves while nobody's looking at it.
- **Arch-enemies face off first** (Ten and Mister Sexton, Twelve and Missy, Amy and the Weeping Angle…), and signature moves where the show has one: Four's jelly babies, Eleven's fez, Jack dropping dead and gasping back, Rose's Bad Wolf glow.
- **Weapons from the show**: the Snakeskin Master spits venom, the Spy Master carries the tissue compression eliminator, Dalicks exterminate, and the Dalick Emperor never fires at all.
- **Clawd Royale**: Doctor Clawd's cast joins everyone else's in one scene when you pick Clawd Royale.
- **A trailer of its own**, at the top of this page.
